Benefits you feel immediately

Recello doesn't just "answer the phone" — it changes how your business handles every single call, order and appointment from the very first day.

⚡ From day one: there is no installation, no hardware and no training to plan. You connect a number, share your business details and Recello starts working — today, not next month.

No lost calls

Every customer gets an answer instantly, at any time — including evenings, weekends and holidays. A missed call is a lost job, and before Recello those jobs simply disappeared. Now the phone is always answered.

  • 24/7 answering in a natural, human voice
  • Callers never hear "please call back during office hours"
  • Every missed after-hours call becomes a booked job or a recorded enquiry

She remembers everything from chat

Tell her once — she knows it on every call. You type your price list, opening hours, services and special offers into the chat, and the receptionist uses them to answer accurately on every single call.

  • No repeating yourself to staff over and over
  • Consistent, correct answers about prices and hours
  • Changes apply instantly — type it, and the next caller already knows

Full details in the chat guide.

Takes orders and books appointments

Recello doesn't just take a message — it takes the order or books the appointment while you do your job. When a customer asks for a quote, a service slot or an emergency visit, it handles the whole booking on the spot.

  • Orders are saved to your Library immediately
  • Appointments are booked and conflicts are handled politely
  • You review everything later — nothing is decided without you

See how it works in orders & appointments.

No installation, no maintenance

There is no hardware to buy, no software to update and nothing to babysit. Recello is cloud-based: it updates itself, never sleeps and never takes a day off.

  • Start immediately — no setup project, no waiting
  • Zero maintenance on your side
  • Works on your phone, tablet or computer via the mobile app

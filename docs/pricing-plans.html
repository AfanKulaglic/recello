What counts towards minutes?

Minutes are counted for the actual duration of calls the receptionist handles (from answering to the end of the conversation). Very short calls under a few seconds are not charged. Idle time when nobody speaks is counted lightly, and the receptionist politely ends calls that go nowhere.

Credits — how they work

Every plan includes minutes. When you need more, you buy prepaid credits — 1 credit = 1 minute of AI answering. Credits never expire, work on any plan, and calls are never cut off if your balance runs out mid-call.

What if I exceed my minutes?

  • Calls are never cut off — the receptionist always finishes the conversation.
  • When your included minutes run out, the next calls automatically use your prepaid credits (1 credit = 1 minute).
  • You get a notification in the app and by email before your balance runs low, so there are no surprises.
  • If you have no credits left, we let you know and you can top up in seconds — a call is never interrupted.
💡 Tip: Credits never expire, so you can buy a larger pack once and use it as needed. The app shows your exact usage and remaining balance so you always know where you stand.

Changing plans

You can change your plan at any time:

  • Upgrading — instant, the difference is prorated.
  • Downgrading — from the next billing period.

Which plan should I pick?

Think about how many calls you receive and whether you want the extra intelligence:

Choose Start (£19) if…
  • You receive a handful of calls a day
  • You already know your prices and services
  • You mainly need reliable 24/7 answering
  • You are testing the service first
Choose Business (£39) if…
  • You receive a steady flow of calls and want credits as you grow
  • You want the receptionist to learn from your website automatically
  • You want smart call transfer and statistics
  • You have 2 numbers or a second location
❓ Still not sure? Start with Start, then upgrade after a month — upgrading is instant and the price difference is prorated. See Payment & billing for details.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a satisfaction guarantee?

Yes — every plan is covered by a 14-day satisfaction guarantee on your first 20 answered calls. If you're not satisfied, we refund your plan fee minus any call minutes already used at the standard credit rate (£0.70/min) — you only pay for the calls you actually made. Identity verification is required before payment (see below).

Why do I need to verify my identity?

To stop misuse of the guarantee and keep the service safe for real customers. You photograph both sides of your ID and a proof of address right on our website — this takes about 3 minutes. Without verification you cannot pay or activate a plan. Start at identity verification.

How do I pay?

Monthly, by card, through our secure checkout (Lemon Squeezy). You receive an invoice by email after every payment. Full details in Payment & billing.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — no contract commitment and no penalties. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period. See Payment & billing.

Are prices in £ including VAT?

Prices are shown before VAT. Because we use a Merchant of Record, the correct tax for your country is calculated and shown at checkout — the price you see is what you pay. See Payment & billing.

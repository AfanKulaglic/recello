Identity verification

We take our 14-day satisfaction guarantee seriously, so every customer must verify their identity before paying. The whole process takes about 3 minutes and is done directly on our website.

⚠️ Important: There is no anonymous sign-up and no free trial without verification. Without a valid ID and proof of residence you cannot pay or activate a plan.

Required documents

You need to photograph (with your phone camera — the website opens it automatically) or upload:

  1. Identity card — front side (national ID card or passport photo page)
  2. Identity card — back side (with the issuing authority and number)
  3. Proof of residence — a utility bill (electricity, gas or water) or a bank statement, no older than 3 months, showing your name and residential address
✅ What's accepted as proof of residence: electricity/gas/water bill, council tax bill, or a recent bank statement — any of these with your name and address is fine.

How it works

  1. Go to the identity verification page
  2. Fill in your details (full name as on your ID, email, phone, address, company)
  3. Choose your plan (Start £19/mo or Business £39/mo)
  4. Photograph both sides of your ID and your proof of residence — on a phone the camera opens automatically
  5. Confirm the consent checkbox and submit

What happens after you submit?

  • We review your documents within 1 working day
  • Once approved, we email you from lumionoai@gmail.com with payment details and activation steps
  • If anything is unclear, we contact you on the phone number you provided
  • Your 14-day satisfaction guarantee (your first 20 answered calls) starts from activation

Privacy

Your documents are used only for identity verification, stored confidentially and never shared with third parties — in line with UK GDPR. If you prefer, you can also send the documents by email or by post; just contact us first.

📎 Tip: Photos should be sharp, well-lit and show the whole document. Total size of all three files must stay under 10 MB.

Mobile app

Everything you need is in your pocket. The app lets you talk to the receptionist, manage your business and track calls, orders and appointments.

Tab overview

💬 Chat

Talk to the receptionist: teach her about your business, send links, rules and temporary notes. Everything you write she uses on calls.

🏢 Company

Company profile: description, opening hours, contacts, services and prices. Here you'll also find Orders, Appointments and Temporary notes.

📞 Calls

Call history with conversation transcripts. See what the customer asked and how the receptionist answered.

⚙️ Settings

Your account, the companies you belong to, language and other settings.

The Company tab — in detail

The Company tab is your command centre:

  • Company information — description, opening hours, phone, email, address, website
  • Services and products — a list with prices and descriptions, by category
  • Temporary notes — notes valid until a date (e.g. "we're closed today")
  • Orders — a list with statuses, manual adding, changing statuses
  • Appointments — booked appointments with statuses

How to add a product or service?

Three ways:

  1. In the Company tab — add the name, price and description manually.
  2. Through chat — write e.g. "add a service: camera installation, £50" and the receptionist will enter it.
  3. With your website — send the link and she extracts products and prices herself.
💡 Note: When you get a price list from your website, check in the Company tab that all prices were entered correctly — sometimes some products have no price on the site.

Sign-in and users

The app can be used by the business owner and employees you give access to. Employees can use chat and manage orders and appointments, while the owner also manages the company profile and users.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in?

You receive an invitation from the business owner or register with your business email. If you have issues, contact us.

Where do I see what the receptionist remembered?

Everything she remembered is shown in the Company tab — services, prices, information and temporary notes.

Do I need a special device?

No — the app works on any smartphone (Android and iPhone).

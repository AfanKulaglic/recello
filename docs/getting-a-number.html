How to get a phone number

Recello is a software service — the AI receptionist that answers your phone. The number itself belongs to you: you can use a number you already have, or get a new one from any licensed provider you choose (for example DIDWW, Twilio, or your own telephone company). We'll help you set it up, step by step — it does not have to be DIDWW.

💰 The price stays the same. Your Recello plan covers the AI receptionist service: Start £19/mo or Business £39/mo. Getting or providing your own number does not change your plan price — DIDWW (or your chosen provider) bills you directly for the number, not us.
📞 What does a number cost? A UK number from DIDWW costs roughly £1–£3 per month (depending on the type — for example a London 020 number), billed to you directly by DIDWW. Your Recello plan price stays exactly the same.
🛠 Need help setting this up? Send us a short message and we'll guide you through it — usually done in ~10 minutes.

Option 1 — I already have a business number

If you already have a phone number (a landline, mobile or a VoIP/DID number), you can connect it to Recello:

  1. Tell us the number and who your current provider is.
  2. We guide you through the simplest way to route it to the receptionist (usually a quick forwarding setting, or connecting your existing SIP trunk).
  3. You confirm the change with your provider (or we do it together).
  4. From that moment the receptionist answers your number.
⚠️ Note: Forwarding an existing number can take 1–3 working days depending on your provider. Until then you can use a temporary number while we test.

Option 2 — I need a new number (via a licensed provider)

If you don't have a number yet, the fastest way is to get one directly from any licensed provider of your choice — we commonly recommend DIDWW because it's simple and works well, but you can equally use Twilio, Telnyx, or your existing telephone provider as long as it supports call routing. The number is registered on your name and billed to you by that provider — not by Recello. Below we show the steps for DIDWW as an example:

  1. Create a DIDWW account: go to didww.com → Sign up and confirm your email. You'll need your company name and address.
  2. Add credit: DIDWW is prepaid. In Billing → Add Funds, top up a small amount (for example £5–£10). Setup and monthly costs are a few pounds per number.
  3. Order your number: in the panel go to Order DID → choose United Kingdom, pick the type you want (for example a London 020 number, a national 03 number, or a mobile 07), and pick an available number.
  4. Set the routing: choose how calls should reach Recello — we provide you with the exact SIP trunk details (or a forwarding target) to paste into the routing settings, such as Forward to or SIP destination.
  5. Tell us your number: that's it — once the number is live, let us know and we connect it to your receptionist in your account.
🛟 No number and no time? We're happy to walk you through it — usually ~10 minutes. Email help@recello.co.uk and we'll guide you step by step. Getting a number from DIDWW is billed to you by DIDWW; your Recello plan price is unchanged.

What happens with calls?

Once your number is connected to Recello:

  • The receptionist answers and handles the conversation 24/7
  • If the customer asks for you, the call is transferred to the number you set in your company profile (owner contact phone)
  • You see every call in the app with the conversation transcript

Frequently asked questions about numbers

Does Recello sell me the number?

No. Recello is a software service — the AI receptionist. The number is yours, either a number you already own or one you order directly from a licensed provider (such as DIDWW, Twilio or your telephone company), who bills you for it. Recello bills you only for the receptionist plan.

Must the number be from DIDWW?

No. DIDWW is the provider we most often recommend because it's simple and works well, but you can use any licensed provider whose numbers support call routing (for example Twilio, Telnyx, or your existing telephone provider). We'll help you connect whichever one you choose.

Can I keep my number if I cancel?

Yes — the number is yours. If it's registered with your provider (such as DIDWW), you keep it and can use it however you like after you cancel Recello.

Can I have more than one number?

Yes. You can connect several numbers to the same receptionist, or a different receptionist for each line. Multiple numbers are supported in the Business and Enterprise plans.

Is the number geographically limited?

No — customers can call you from anywhere in the country, and you choose the area code you want (for example London 020, national 03, or mobile 07).

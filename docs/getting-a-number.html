How to get a DID number

DID (Direct Inward Dialing) is your business phone number that customers call. There are three ways to get one — choose the one that suits you best.

Option 1 — A new business number (fastest)

  1. Contact us via the contact form or by phone and say you'd like a new number.
  2. Tell us which city / area code you'd like the number from (e.g. London 020).
  3. We check availability and assign you the number — usually the same day.
  4. The number is automatically connected to your receptionist.
✅ Included: A new number comes with every plan with no additional activation costs — the number, app and receptionist are all included in your monthly price.
💰 Extra numbers: Each additional number beyond your plan is billed at a small transparent rate (~£3/mo per number, one-time setup ~£2.50). You can add or remove numbers at any time — there is no contract and no hidden fee.

Option 2 — Connecting an existing number

If you already have a business number (e.g. a landline with your provider), we can redirect it to Recello:

  1. Give us the number and details about your current provider.
  2. We prepare the forwarding to our exchange.
  3. You confirm the forwarding with your provider (or we do it together).
  4. From that moment the receptionist answers your number.
⚠️ Note: Forwarding an existing number can take 1–3 working days depending on your provider. Until then you can use a temporary number.

Option 3 — Number without forwarding (promo/test)

For testing and demonstrations we often use temporary numbers that require no changes at your provider. Ideal for getting started.

What happens with calls?

When a customer calls your DID number:

  • The receptionist answers and handles the conversation 24/7
  • If the customer asks for you, the call is transferred to the number you set in your company profile (owner contact phone)
  • You see every call in the app with the conversation transcript

Frequently asked questions about numbers

Can I have more than one number?

Yes. Each number can be connected to the same or a different receptionist. Multiple numbers are included in the Business and Enterprise plans.

Can I keep my number if I cancel?

Yes — you can port the number back to your provider or keep it as your property.

Is the number geographically limited?

No — customers can call you from anywhere in the country, and you can choose the area code you want.

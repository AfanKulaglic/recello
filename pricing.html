Questions about pricing?

Frequently asked questions

Is the phone number included?

Nothing extra — every plan includes a UK business number, provisioned via our partner DIDWW and set up for you. Prefer your existing number? Forward it instead — see the setup guide.

Is there a satisfaction guarantee?

Yes — every plan is covered by our 14-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy, we refund your plan fee minus any call usage at the standard credit rate — you only pay for the calls you actually made.

What about call minutes?

Call minutes are handled under your plan (with optional extra minutes if you ever need more). Your included UK number is provisioned via our partner DIDWW as part of the plan — no separate number bill.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, with no contract commitment. Cancel whenever you want — no penalties, no questions asked.

Need help setting up?

Choosing a number or connecting one can feel fiddly — but you're not on your own. Send us a short message and we'll guide you through it step by step.

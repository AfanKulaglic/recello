Questions about pricing?

Frequently asked questions

How much does the number cost?

A business number is included in every plan. If you already have a number you use, we can connect it.

Is there a satisfaction guarantee?

Yes — every plan is covered by our 14-day satisfaction guarantee on your first 20 answered calls. If you're not happy, we refund your plan fee minus any call minutes already used at the standard credit rate (£0.70/min) — you only pay for the calls you actually made. Verification of identity is required first.

How do credits work?

1 credit = 1 minute of answering. Each call deducts ~1 credit per minute from your balance, you see your usage live, and we warn you before you run out. Calls are never cut off.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, with no contract commitment. Cancel whenever you want — no penalties, no questions asked.

Why do you ask for my ID?

To stop anyone abusing the guarantee and to keep every customer safe. We verify both sides of your ID and a proof of address before you can pay — and we never share the documents.

The math

A single missed call can cost more than a month of Recello

Industry research consistently shows that around 60% of callers who reach voicemail or an unanswered line hang up and call someone else — and up to a third never call back at all. For a business that receives, say, 10 calls a day, that is potentially 6 customers a day going to your competitors.

Think about what one job is worth to you. A plumber's average job might be £150–£300. A salon booking is worth £40–£80, and a regular client is worth thousands over a year. If you miss just 2–3 jobs a month, that is already more than a full year of Recello at £19/month.

Here is the honest calculation: 10 missed calls per day × 60% who go elsewhere = 6 lost leads a day = 180 lost leads a month. Even if you would only have closed 10% of them, that is 18 lost jobs a month. What would 18 extra jobs be worth to you?

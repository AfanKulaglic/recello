Why it matters

Small businesses lose customers every time the phone rings and nobody answers

If you run a small business in the UK — a plumber, a salon, a builder, a shop — the phone is still how customers reach you. But you cannot be there to answer it every time. You are on a job, in a meeting, driving, or asleep at 2am. And every call that goes to voicemail or rings out is a customer who simply calls your competitor next.

Recello is an AI phone receptionist built for exactly this. She answers your business number in a natural voice, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. She tells callers about your services and prices, takes orders, books appointments, and transfers urgent calls straight to your mobile. She never puts anyone on hold and she never forgets a conversation.

The best part? She costs less than a single day of a human receptionist's salary. Plans start at £19/month, there is no contract, and setup takes less than a day — you can send your website link or simply tell her about your business in the app chat.

Our story

We know what a missed call costs

Recello was born from a simple problem: businesses lose customers while they're in a meeting, in the shop, out on the job or simply asleep. The phone rings — and nobody answers. We built a receptionist that always answers, always responds accurately and always remembers what matters.

Powered by artificial intelligence, natural speech and the data you type in chat — she does the job of your best employee, without breaks, without holidays and without bad days.

  • Our mission — that no business ever misses a call.
  • Simplicity — manage the conversation like sending a message.
  • Technology that understands your language and your industry.

What exactly happens when a customer calls?

1. Answers and listens

The receptionist introduces herself with your company name and listens carefully to what the customer needs — without interrupting.

2. Understands and answers

Uses your data from the profile and chat to answer accurately — prices, opening hours, services, discounts.
